🌟 Privacy Policy

Bijak Belajar – ANF Studio

Effective Date: 29 October 2026
Developer: ANF Studio
App Name: Bijak Belajar
Package: com.anfstudio.bijakbelajar

Short version: Bijak Belajar does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data from you or your child. There is no account and no login. The app uses the microphone only when your child taps the speak button to practise saying letters aloud — your device's own speech service turns that speech into text, and Bijak Belajar itself never records, saves, or receives the audio.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. Bijak Belajar does not collect, use, upload, or share any personal data such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, or advertising/device identifiers. We do not create user profiles and we do not track your child's activity across apps or websites.

2. Microphone & Voice (Pronunciation Practice)

Some activities let your child say a letter, number, or word out loud so the app can give friendly pronunciation feedback. To do this, the app asks for permission to use the microphone and uses your device's built-in speech recognition.

  • The microphone is used only when your child taps the speak button in a speaking activity. Before the first request, the app shows a grown-up a short explanation, and the microphone is only used if permission is granted.
  • Speech is turned into text by your device's built-in speech-recognition service (for example, Google on Android or Apple on iOS) so the app can check the pronunciation. Depending on your device and its settings, that service may process the audio on the device or send it to its provider's servers to recognise the words, under that provider's own privacy terms.
  • Bijak Belajar itself does not record, store, or receive your child's voice. No audio files are created or kept by the app, and nothing is sent to ANF Studio.
  • You can use the rest of the app without the microphone by simply declining the permission.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA / PDPA / Google Play Families)

Bijak Belajar is designed for children (approximately ages 3–7) and is intended to be used with a parent or guardian. We are committed to protecting children's privacy. We do not:

  • Collect any personal information from children
  • Display advertisements of any kind
  • Include chat, messaging, or social-networking features
  • Use tracking, profiling, or behavioural analytics

This app is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA 2010), and the Google Play Families Policy.

4. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

The app saves only basic learning settings and progress (such as language preference, sound on/off, star count, and level) and any coloring pictures your child chooses to save. All of it is kept in the app's private storage on the device only and is never uploaded. It stays there until you delete it:

  • Progress: Parent Settings → Reset Progress erases stars, badges, and completed lessons.
  • Pictures: open a saved picture in the coloring gallery and tap delete.
  • Everything: uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device.

Because ANF Studio never receives this data, we hold no copy that would need deleting.

5. Keeping Data Safe

Bijak Belajar never sends this data anywhere, so it stays protected by your device's own app sandboxing and screen lock. ANF Studio does not run any servers that store information about you or your child. On Android the app opts out of cloud backup; on iPhone and iPad, a backup you make of the whole device (for example to iCloud) may include the app's data, protected by your own account.

6. Sharing a Coloring Picture (Optional)

In the coloring activity, a child can ask to share a picture they coloured. A grown-up must first answer a simple maths question, and only then does the device's own share menu open (for example, to save the image or send it via another app you choose). Bijak Belajar does not upload, receive, or keep a copy of anything you share, and does not choose the recipient.

7. Internet & Network

The app itself makes no network requests and does not include the Android Internet permission — all learning content is bundled inside the app and works offline. Speech recognition and text-to-speech are separate services built into your device; they may use the internet according to your device's own settings.

8. Third-Party Services

Bijak Belajar does not use any third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or data collection. The only third-party components used are your device's built-in Text-to-Speech and Speech Recognition services, which are provided by your device's operating system and are governed by that provider's own privacy terms.

9. Permissions We Request

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) – only for the optional pronunciation-practice activities described in Section 2.

The app does not request access to the camera, contacts, call logs, location (GPS), or your files and external storage.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage parents to review this page periodically.
