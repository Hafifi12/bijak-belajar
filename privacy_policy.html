🌟 Privacy Policy

Bijak Belajar – ANF Studio

Effective Date: 10 August 2026
Developer: ANF Studio
App Name: Bijak Belajar
Package: com.anfstudio.bijakbelajar

Short version: Bijak Belajar does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data from you or your child. There is no account and no login. The app uses the microphone only so your child can practise saying letters and words aloud — that audio is processed by your device and is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. Bijak Belajar does not collect, use, upload, or share any personal data such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, or advertising/device identifiers. We do not create user profiles and we do not track your child's activity across apps or websites.

2. Microphone & Voice (Pronunciation Practice)

Some activities let your child say a letter, number, or word out loud so the app can give friendly pronunciation feedback. To do this, the app asks for permission to use the microphone and uses your device's built-in speech recognition.

  • The microphone is used only while a speaking activity is open, after permission is granted.
  • Voice input is turned into text by your device's own speech-recognition service so the app can check the pronunciation.
  • The app does not record, store, or transmit your child's voice. No audio files are created or kept, and nothing is sent to ANF Studio or any third party.
  • You can use the rest of the app without the microphone by simply declining the permission.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA / PDPA / Google Play Families)

Bijak Belajar is designed for children (approximately ages 3–7) and is intended to be used with a parent or guardian. We are committed to protecting children's privacy. We do not:

  • Collect any personal information from children
  • Display advertisements of any kind
  • Include chat, messaging, or social-networking features
  • Use tracking, profiling, or behavioural analytics

This app is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA 2010), and the Google Play Families Policy.

4. Data Storage

The app saves only basic learning settings and progress (such as language preference, sound on/off, star count, and level) using local device storage only (SharedPreferences). This data stays on the device, is never uploaded, and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

5. Sharing a Coloring Picture (Optional)

In the coloring activity, a child (with a parent's help) can choose to share a picture they coloured. Sharing is always initiated by the user and uses your device's own share menu (for example, to save the image or send it via another app you choose). Bijak Belajar does not upload, receive, or keep a copy of anything you share, and does not choose the recipient.

6. Internet & Network

The app itself makes no network requests and does not include the Android Internet permission — all learning content is bundled inside the app and works offline. Speech recognition and text-to-speech are handled by your device's built-in system services.

7. Third-Party Services

Bijak Belajar does not use any third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or data collection. The only third-party components used are your device's built-in Text-to-Speech and Speech Recognition services, which are provided by your device's operating system and are governed by that provider's own privacy terms.

8. Permissions We Request

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) – only for the optional pronunciation-practice activities described in Section 2.

The app does not request access to the camera, contacts, call logs, location (GPS), or your files and external storage.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage parents to review this page periodically.
