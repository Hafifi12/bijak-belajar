🌟 Privacy Policy

Bijak Belajar – ANF Studio

Effective Date: 4 May 2026
Developer: ANF Studio
App Name: Bijak Belajar
Package: com.anfstudio.bijakbelajar

Short version: Bijak Belajar does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data from you or your child. The app works fully offline and requires no account or login.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. Bijak Belajar does not collect, use, save, or share any personal data including names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, photos, or any device identifiers.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA / PDPA)

Bijak Belajar is designed for children aged 3–7. We are fully committed to protecting children's privacy online. We do not:

  • Collect any personal information from children
  • Display advertisements of any kind
  • Include social features, chat, or user-generated content
  • Link to external websites or third-party services
  • Use tracking or behavioural analytics

This app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA 2010).

3. Data Storage

The app saves only basic settings (such as language preference, sound on/off, and star count) using local device storage only (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

4. Internet Access

Bijak Belajar does not require an internet connection and does not make any network requests. All content is bundled within the app.

5. Third-Party Services

Bijak Belajar does not use any third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or data collection. The only third-party component used is the device's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine, which runs entirely on-device and does not transmit data.

6. Permissions

The app does not request any sensitive device permissions. It does not access:

  • Camera or microphone
  • Contacts or call logs
  • Location (GPS)
  • Files or external storage
  • Internet / network

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage parents to review this page periodically.
